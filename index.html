Questions people ask

Straight answers about what this tool is and isn’t.

Can MediPredictAI diagnose me?

No. It gives general information and possible explanations, which can be wrong. Only a clinician who examines you can diagnose a condition.

What happens to what I submit?

Your text and files are sent to an AI model to write your answer. This page doesn’t save them. Avoid sharing your full name, ID numbers or other identifying details.

Which reports can I upload?

Photos or screenshots of reports, and plain text files. PDFs aren’t supported yet, so paste the text into the symptoms box instead.

When should I see a doctor instead?

If symptoms are severe, getting worse, or lasting longer than expected, see a doctor. For emergencies, call your local emergency number immediately.
